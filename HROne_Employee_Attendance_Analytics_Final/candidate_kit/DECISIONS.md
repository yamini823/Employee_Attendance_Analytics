# DECISIONS.md

1. **Indexes:** Created unique `employees(emp_code)` and `attendance_logs(emp_code, date)` for identity and race safety. Added `employees(joined_on)` and `employees(department, joined_on)` for headcount filtering without collection scans, `employees(department, emp_code)` and `attendance_logs(date, emp_code)` for sorted pagination, `attendance_logs(emp_code, punch_in)` for punch-out, and `attendance_logs(date, late_minutes)` for leaderboard scans. Rejected indexing `status` alone due to low cardinality.
2. **Punch-in race:** Both concurrent requests attempt `insert_one()`. The compound unique index on `(emp_code, date)` serializes insertion at the storage engine: exactly one document commits, while the concurrent request raises a MongoDB `DuplicateKeyError`, intercepted to return HTTP 409 Conflict without dirty writes.
3. **Ties:** Using `$setWindowFields` with `$rank`, tied employees share ranks with skipped subsequent ranks (1, 2, 2, 4). Filtering on `rank <= limit` rather than array slicing ensures all tied employees at the cutoff are included, sorted deterministically by `emp_code` ascending.
4. **Headcount:** The aggregation pipeline anchors on `employees` (filtering `joined_on <= period_end`), groups by department to record total headcount, and left-joins (`$lookup`) attendance logs. Employees with zero logs preserve department headcount without being pruned.
5. **100x Data Scaling:** Maintain an incremental pre-aggregated daily rollup collection for department metrics, updating incrementally during punch-out/regularization to replace large multi-stage joins with point reads.
